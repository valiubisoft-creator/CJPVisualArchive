# CJP Protest — Public-Access Image Set

Images of the **Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)** protests in India (2026), collected 22 Oct 2026 for the CJP Protest Visualiser.

**Context.** The CJP is a Gen Z/student-led movement named after Chief Justice of India Surya Kant's 15 May 2026 remark calling some activists and unemployed youth "cockroaches". Its protests at Jantar Mantar, New Delhi (6 Jun – 25 Jul 2026) targeted the NEET-UG 2026 paper leak and CBSE on-screen-marking failures, and demanded the Education Minister's resignation (he resigned around 25 Jul). In October 2026 it led near-daily protests over the voter-roll revision, demanding the Chief Election Commissioner's resignation. Background: [Wikipedia — Cockroach Janta Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cockroach_Janta_Party), [Wikipedia — 2026 Delhi Jantar Mantar protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Delhi_Jantar_Mantar_protests), [Al Jazeera, 19 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/19/indian-court-extends-detention-of-cockroach-leaders).

## How to credit (license obligations)

- **CC BY-SA 4.0** (37 photos): credit the author, link the license, note any changes. **Share-alike**: if you publish an *adapted* image (crop, blur, colour grade), the adapted image must also be CC BY-SA 4.0. Credit format: `Photo: Sumita Roy Dutta / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 4.0`.
- **CC BY 4.0** (4 flag PNGs + `cjp_logo_variant-c.png`): credit the author and link the license. Share-alike does not apply.
- **CC0 / Public Domain**: no credit legally required, but cite the source anyway.
- **Logo caution**: Commons notes the copyright status only. The CJP name and emblem may still raise trademark or personality issues, so use them descriptively (documentation), not as an endorsement.

## Ethics notes (per `docs/SOURCING_TAKEDOWN.md` / `docs/ANONYMIZATION.md`)

- These files are **downloaded copies**, unlike the project's embed-only video approach. That is allowed by their licenses, but it means the project now holds the images and is responsible for them.
- **Most protest photos show identifiable faces of young protesters**, see the *Identifiable faces* column. Given the October arrests and the DPDPA position in `SOURCING_TAKEDOWN.md`, consider blurring faces before publishing. Remember that a blurred CC BY-SA image must itself be released CC BY-SA.
- **Never** run face-matching across this set.
- **Flags on individual files:**
  - `bengaluru_dinkan-poster-*` — both are under an **active Commons deletion request (Aug 2026)**. Check their status before use; links could break.
  - `solidarity_los-angeles_2026-07.jpg` — the EXIF date reads 2019-07-24, but the banner in frame says *July 24, 2026*. The camera clock was almost certainly wrong; the date on the banner is correct.
  - `jantar-mantar_protest_2026-07-21.jpg` and `jantar-mantar_slogans_2026-07-21.jpg` aren't yet in the Commons protest category. They come from the same uploader and shoot as the other 21 July files.
- **Excluded on purpose:** `File:CJP, India.jpg` (logo of *Citizens for Justice and Peace*, an unrelated NGO) and `File:CJP-flag.svg` (flag of *Congress Jananayaka Peravai*, an unrelated party).
