“Cockroaches.”

In open court, the Chief Justice of India called some unemployed young people and activists cockroaches and “parasites of society”. He later said he meant people with fake degrees. The next day a joke party was born online. Within ten weeks a minister had resigned, and the state had muted it, cut the internet around it and detained its members by the thousand.

    0715 May – 21 Oct

    Thirteen hands on one protest.

    Every body with a documented role in the response, from riot police to the telecom companies. Solid red lines show restrictions. Courts, which both restricted and restored, are dashed.

    On the street

    Police in three cities, the Rapid Action Force, the CRPF and the Delhi Metro, which shut up to 18 stations a day.

    Online

    The Home Ministry, the IT ministry, the Intelligence Bureau and the telecom operators who switched off the signal, usually without a published order.

    In court

    The Delhi High Court restored the account, and also ordered a hunger striker removed from the site.

    In power

    A minister who called the followers foreign, a minister who resigned, and the Commission whose rolls started the second wave.

    Method

    A prototype in interactive storytelling, built with anime.js v4. Figures come from the reporting listed here. Counts are as reported, not independently verified. Where the state, the records and the movement disagree, all three are shown and coloured by who said them.

    The form borrows from investigative platforms by Forensic Architecture, Bellingcat and SITU Research, which tie every mark to a source, and from the visual essays of The Pudding. The blackout and network diagrams are schematic, not maps. The phone is an illustration.
