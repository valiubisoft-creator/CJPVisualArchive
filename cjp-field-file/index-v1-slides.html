Cockroach  /  a field file CJP protests · India · 15 May – 21 Oct 2026
Supreme Court of India · 15 May 2026

“Parasites of society.”

In open court the Chief Justice of India called some unemployed youth and activists “cockroaches”. (He later said he meant people with fake degrees.) The next day, Abhijeet Dipke launched the Cockroach Janta Party online, as a joke.

Ten weeks later the Education Minister had resigned. Along the way the movement was muted online, cut off and detained in the thousands. This file follows the people, the agencies and the numbers.
